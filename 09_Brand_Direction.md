# BranchCast — Brand Direction

## Core Idea
**Sound, under control.**

BranchCast should feel like a reliable control room for a living brand experience: warm enough to reflect audio and atmosphere, but disciplined enough for daily business operations.

It is not a consumer music app, a DJ dashboard, or a podcast-template clone.

## Visual Positioning
| Attribute | Direction |
|---|---|
| Product personality | Calm, confident, precise, contemporary |
| Primary feeling | Controlled energy |
| Emotional balance | Premium and warm, never playful or loud |
| Dashboard character | Operational SaaS with an editorial visual finish |
| Audio expression | Subtle signal, rhythm, playback, and timing cues |
| Avoid | Neon gradients, nightclub imagery, heavy waveforms, microphones as decoration, excessive glassmorphism |

## Reference Influence
### Soundly
Adopt the warm-dark audio atmosphere, tactile accent colour, large confident typography, and layered information cards.

Do not adopt the consumer podcast language, host photography, listener counters, or orange-heavy page treatment.

### PodCastle
Adopt the restrained dark-space composition and the use of signal-like vertical rhythm as a very subtle decorative device.

Do not adopt the entertainment-first visual hierarchy or make decorative equalizer bars a dominant product motif.

### Parley
Adopt the generous spacing, quiet off-white surfaces, strong black-and-orange call-to-action hierarchy, rounded large containers, and premium product confidence.

Do not adopt its exact layouts, brand expression, or marketing-only composition.

## Final Direction
The BranchCast dashboard uses:
- warm off-white workspace surfaces for long-term readability;
- a deep graphite navigation shell;
- signal orange only for decisive action, on-air, and priority moments;
- structured cards and panels with low visual noise;
- one or two rich dark surfaces for high-value operational context, not an entire dark dashboard;
- concise typography with high information clarity.

## Logo Direction
The logo should be a restrained wordmark, initially using text only:
- BranchCast
- no microphone, headphones, antenna, or radio icon by default;
- a minimal signal or branch-like mark may be explored later, but is not required for the prototype;
- do not create a decorative logo before the product interface is approved.

## Imagery Direction
The core dashboard needs no stock photography.

Where visual media is required:
- use abstract, subtle audio-signal forms or contextual business-location imagery;
- keep real people and studio/podcast images for a later marketing website;
- use no visual that suggests BranchCast owns music rights or streams a commercial music catalog.

## Dashboard Layout Character
- Dark sidebar on the left in English/LTR and right in Arabic/RTL.
- Warm light canvas with a 12-column desktop grid.
- Compact, calm top bar.
- Large page title only where it helps task orientation.
- Overview can include one dark “Live Network” or “On Air” feature panel.
- Supporting cards are neutral, flat, and data-focused.
- Attention states use structured action cards, never alarmist banners.

## Tone of Voice
| Situation | Tone |
|---|---|
| Normal operations | Direct and calm |
| Player issue | Specific, helpful, non-alarmist |
| Empty state | Encouraging and practical |
| Confirmation | Clear about scope and consequence |
| Prototype-only behaviour | Transparent, concise |

## Example UI Language
- Player online
- Needs attention
- Last seen 4 min ago
- Schedule ready to publish
- No player paired yet
- Review changes
- Save draft
- Add location

## Brand Guardrails
- No gradients in operational UI, except an optional extremely subtle ambient wash in marketing-only surfaces.
- No decorative waveforms in tables, forms, or navigation.
- No all-caps interface copy.
- No more than one prominent orange action per view.
- Use shadows for elevation, not as decoration.
- Prioritize hierarchy, spacing, and status clarity before visual effects.
