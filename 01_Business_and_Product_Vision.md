# BranchCast — Business & Product Vision

## The Problem
Multi-branch businesses often leave audio decisions to each individual branch. The result is inconsistent music, personal accounts and devices, missed promotions, no central visibility, and no reliable way to confirm what customers are hearing.

## The Opportunity
Audio can become a controlled brand channel: a company chooses approved audio, schedules it centrally, adds its own short messages, and monitors branch playback without contacting every site.

## Value Proposition
**BranchCast gives multi-location businesses one place to manage approved in-store audio, promotional messages, and player health.**

## Initial Ideal Customer Profile
| Attribute | Initial fit |
|---|---|
| Business type | Café, restaurant, retail, gym, salon, or hospitality |
| Locations | 2–30 locations |
| Buyer | Operations Manager, Marketing Manager, Owner, or IT lead |
| Need | Consistent atmosphere, branch control, and campaign execution |
| Current workaround | Staff phones, personal music accounts, USB drives, or unmonitored speakers |

## Jobs to Be Done
- When I launch a promotion, I want a message scheduled across selected branches so the offer is communicated consistently.
- When a branch goes silent or plays the wrong content, I want to know quickly and resolve it remotely.
- When we open a new branch, I want to connect a player and publish approved audio without recreating everything.
- When I manage a brand, I want the customer experience to feel consistent regardless of branch.

## Why BranchCast Can Matter
- Replaces uncontrolled local playback with approved, centralized control.
- Separates brand audio operations from staff personal accounts.
- Makes promotional audio measurable and schedulable.
- Supports location and zone-level control rather than one stream for everyone.

## Commercial Model — Future Validation
The prototype is free to build and test. A future commercial model may include:
- monthly subscription by active location or audio zone;
- higher tiers for multi-brand organizations, advanced reporting, and support;
- optional managed content and professional audio-message production.

No pricing is approved in Phase 1.

## Success Signals for an MVP Pilot
- A test organization can add locations and player zones without assistance.
- A schedule can be published to a selected zone.
- The dashboard can show a credible current playback and connection state.
- A manager can identify an offline player and know the next action.
- Users understand the difference between content, campaigns, and schedules.

## Key Risks and How We Handle Them
| Risk | Phase 1 response |
|---|---|
| Music rights | BranchCast only supports customer-owned, licensed, royalty-free, or lawfully approved sources. |
| Free build constraint | No paid APIs, paid catalog, payment gateway, or paid infrastructure in the prototype. |
| Playback reliability | Design for clear status, last-seen time, and future offline strategy; do not pretend offline playback already exists. |
| Scope inflation | Keep the first release to central configuration, status, and basic scheduling. |
| Customer fit | Validate with actual multi-branch operators before investing in advanced integrations. |

## Working Assumptions
These are assumptions, not confirmed facts:
1. The first practical pilot will use one organization with several locations.
2. One location may later contain more than one audio zone.
3. The first MVP will use only approved sample content and/or a user-provided legal stream URL.
4. Desktop is the primary dashboard surface; the Branch Player is designed for a tablet, browser, or small device.
